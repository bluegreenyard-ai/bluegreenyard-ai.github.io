AI Approach

The Blue Green Yard follows a hybrid approach: sensor measurements and weather forecasts are combined with transparent learning algorithms, rule-based safety logic and future machine-learning models.

Sensor Data + Weather Forecast + Historical Events → Learning → Decision Support → Automatic Control
Important principle:
AI supports learning and optimization. Critical pump, valve and overflow functions remain protected by deterministic safety rules.

Already Implemented: Adaptive Inflow Learning

IMPLEMENTED

Online Learning

The system learns continuously from new rain events instead of using permanently fixed rainfall-to-inflow factors.

IMPLEMENTED

Tank-Specific Learning

Each of the four tanks has its own learned relationship between rainfall in millimetres and water inflow in litres.

IMPLEMENTED

Robust Learning

Valid recent events are combined using a robust median to reduce the influence of individual sensor errors.

IMPLEMENTED

Gradual Adaptation

New observations do not immediately replace the existing factor. The model is updated step by step.

What Does the System Learn?

🌦️ 1. Forecast
Predicted rain

How much rain is expected?

🌧️ 2. Real Rain
Measured mm

How much rain actually occurred?

💧 3. Tank Response
Volume change

How many litres actually entered the tank?

🧠 4. New Factor
L/mm

How much water reaches this tank per millimetre of rain?

Measured Rain + Tank Volume Increase → Observed L/mm → Robust Median → Gradual Update
Learning rule:
At least three valid rain events are required before the learned information is used. Very small events, implausible values and events with a tank already at 100 % are excluded from learning.
